    Turn on Uplink in the list that opens. It notices within a few seconds, with no restart.

    Sign in

    Run claude setup-token in Terminal, sign in, and paste the token it prints. Uplink keeps it in your Keychain.

    Run codex login in Terminal and sign in. Codex keeps the credential itself, so there is nothing to paste here.
